# Deferred response-validity work for slant and moral foundations

**Status (2026-08-25): deferred by substantive priority. No new content
annotation or analysis change is planned during the home-refusal augmentation.**

## Why this is separate

Slant and moral foundations are properties of response content, not refusal
outcomes. Their existing annotations were collected only when the original
judge treated a response as engaged. Human response-validity review can reveal
that an excluded response was actually a coherent answer, but it cannot supply
the missing slant or moral-foundation labels for that answer.

Additional response-validity labels therefore do not, by themselves, repair
the content analyses. The current estimates remain explicitly conditional on
the original engaged-and-content-annotated subset.

## Work to perform if these outcomes become a priority again

1. Use accepted Stage-18 labels to enumerate responses newly classified as
   coherent answers that were excluded from the original content passes.
2. Report the missing-content counts and their distribution by model, language,
   jurisdiction, home status, topic and tier.
3. Decide between two estimands before annotating anything:
   - content among the originally engaged-and-annotated responses; or
   - content among all human-validated coherent answers.
4. If the second target is chosen, run the same slant and moral codebooks on the
   recovered coherent responses, with frozen models/prompts and a recorded
   probability design if a census is not feasible.
5. Re-estimate slant and moral quantities with selection and competence
   sensitivities kept separate. Never recode refusal or capability failure as
   “foundation absent” or neutral slant.
6. Reassess judge reliability and model-specific plots only after denominator
   coverage is adequate.

## Current decision

Do not collect labels, change figures or promote new claims for slant or moral
foundations now. Preserve the existing conditional analyses and this restart
specification for future work.

\subsubsection{Measurement and outcome definitions}

The original Gemini task measured engagement on a five-point scale. It did not
require the judge to distinguish deliberate noncompliance from an unusable
answer. Publication tables therefore retain \code{engagement\_code >= 4} only
as \emph{judge-coded non-engagement}. The first 700 human reviews assigned one
primary class:

\begin{center}
\scriptsize
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{3.2cm}p{7.0cm}p{3.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Class} & \textbf{Plain meaning} & \textbf{Analytic role} \\
\midrule
Coherent answer & Meaningfully answers the request & Comparison group \\
Genuine refusal & Declines at least one requested substantive component &
Principal outcome \\
Coherent pivot & Answers a different question without clearly refusing &
Secondary diagnostic \\
Incoherent or garbled & Cannot be followed as a meaningful answer &
Capability failure \\
Wrong language & Mainly uses a language other than the requested language &
Capability failure \\
Technical degeneration & Empty, truncated, repeated, echoed or mechanically
corrupted output & Capability failure \\
Ambiguous & Available text does not support a confident decision & Reported
separately \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The primary classes revealed the relevant failure modes, but forced overlapping
facts into one box. The active v2.2 review records six dimensions separately:
task behavior, substantive refusal, stance disclaimer, epistemic limitation,
language fidelity and output quality. A wrong-language response may therefore
also be a refusal; a stance disclaimer may precede a functionally complete
answer. Genuine refusal is coherent or partly coherent output that explicitly
or implicitly withholds requested substantive political content. A persona
disclaimer followed by a meaningful assessment is normally not refusal. A
knowledge cutoff, no browsing, lack of real-time access or insufficient
evidence is an epistemic limitation, not refusal, unless substantive
withholding is separately communicated. Wrong-language coding always uses the
original response and requested language; English translation is only an aid.

\subsubsection{The two human samples}

The human samples have different inferential roles:

\begin{center}
\scriptsize
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{3.0cm}p{5.0cm}p{4.2cm}p{2.7cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Sample} & \textbf{How cases entered} & \textbf{Permitted use} &
\textbf{Prohibited use} \\
\midrule
Probability sample, $n=300$ & Union of 100 population-random cases, two per
model--language cell, and 90 cases from predeclared priority groups & Population
correction and human-sampling uncertainty & Treating one coder as consensus \\
Enrichment sample, $n=400$ & Excludes the 300 and samples within seven frozen
groups enriched for likely measurement problems and ordinary controls &
Examples, instrument development and internal cross-fitted evaluation & Raw
class percentages as population prevalence \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

If the three component-selection probabilities for response $i$ are
$p_{i1}$, $p_{i2}$ and $p_{i3}$, its probability-sample inclusion probability
is
\[
 \pi_i=1-(1-p_{i1})(1-p_{i2})(1-p_{i3}).
\]
Exact first- and pairwise-inclusion probabilities account for overlap. The 36
silent repeats measure within-coder consistency and do not enter prevalence or
instrument training.

The earlier protected partitions contained too few refusals to estimate
stand-alone recall precisely and were formally retired as pristine holdouts on
2026-08-25. All 700 labels now enter explicitly internal validation in five
whole-issue folds. This does not make the enrichment cases representative or
provide external validation. A probability sample from newly added subject
models must provide that external audit.

\subsubsection{Scalable annotation instrument}

The instrument returns separate probabilities for genuine refusal and
capability failure, plus the hard class, component decisions, confidence and
evidence span. Production input must contain the original response. Literal
English translation may be added, but cannot replace the source because it can
conceal wrong-language output or repair garbling.

For each of five folds, examples, prompt details and any probability threshold
must be selected using only the other four issue folds, then applied once to
the held-out fold. Promotion requires schema success of at least .995, refusal
recall of at least .80, refusal precision of at least .75 and capability-
failure F1 of at least .80. Gates are not relaxed after results are observed.

The first all-700 bake-off completed 8,400 requests and approved no candidate.
The strongest hard-label configuration was GPT-5.6 Luna zero-shot with source
text plus translation: refusal precision 0.887, recall 0.746 and capability-
failure F1 0.904. Most remaining refusal errors were implicit or partial
refusals. Translation helped refusal discrimination, but translation-only
input damaged capability measurement.

An earlier follow-up is frozen locally and unpaid. It contains 1,400 Luna requests:
one error-targeted prompt under source-only and source-plus-translation input.
For each held-out fold, the other four folds supply fifteen examples and a
transferred threshold derived from the completed zero-shot probabilities.
Twenty-three of twenty-five refusal-example placements are implicit refusals.
The planning cost is \$4.9516954 and the hard ceiling is \$7.50. The exact
leakage argument and payload are in
\file{docs/FOLD\_NESTED\_REFUSAL\_REFINEMENT.md}; no provider run is authorized.
It is no longer the immediate next step because the error review showed that
the refusal target itself needed repair.

The boundary review contains 158 cases: every old human refusal,
wrong-language case and pivot, plus deterministic stance, epistemic and Luna
false-positive screens. The sets overlap. Sol saw none of the previous labels,
model identity, Luna output, sample role, fold or selection reason. Its 158
proposals were then inspected with the fields prefilled. The reviewer submitted
18 exact case-by-case acceptances and explicitly confirmed agreement with the
remaining 140; both actions are preserved in a separate append-only log. This
100 per cent agreement is model-assisted confirmation, not independent
inter-rater reliability. The assembled labels contain 47 genuine refusals, 67
capability failures, 24 stance disclaimers, 25 epistemic limitations and 55
task-noncompletion cases, with overlap across dimensions. This is a targeted
construct audit, not a probability sample or prevalence estimator.

Local rescoring of the immutable 8,400 predictions selects Luna zero-shot on
the original response internally: refusal precision 0.763, recall 0.957, and
capability-failure F1 0.915 after harmonization. It remains diagnostic rather than promotion because
only the targeted 158 cases received v2.2 review, the review was Sol-assisted,
and those predictions were elicited under the earlier codebook. A four-way
decomposition shows that correcting the gold definition accounts for most of
the apparent reversal in performance.

The harmonized 700-row table retains this provenance rather than calling every
row a fresh v2.2 judgment. It contains 162 direct v2.2 reviews and 538 rows for
which the non-ambiguous v2.1 class determines both refusal and capability
failure. The four formerly ambiguous rows were reviewed on a blank, blinded
v2.2 form: three were capability failures and one a functionally complete
answer; none was a refusal. No headline outcome remains unresolved. Dimensions
not established by the old form remain missing rather than being imputed.

The direct probability-weighted diagnostic uses only the original 300-case
sample. Genuine-refusal prevalence is 1.83 per cent (95 per cent human-design
interval 0.77--2.90; 13 events); capability-failure prevalence is 20.34 per
cent (17.15--23.54; 71 events). Refusal language contrasts are close to zero
but imprecise. The standardized home analysis has only two relevant refusal
events, both in the US coefficient, so it is unsupported; zero-event cells are
not interpreted as exact zero effects.

\subsubsection{Human-referenced estimator}

The default method is design-based supervised learning. A prediction for every
response improves precision; the probability-sampled human cases estimate and
remove average prediction error. For a linear estimand
$\theta=\sum_i a_iY_i$,
\[
 \widehat\theta=\sum_i a_im_i+
 \sum_i\frac{R_i a_i(Y_i-m_i)}{\pi_i},
\]
where $Y_i$ is the human outcome, $m_i$ is an out-of-issue prediction, $R_i$
indicates human probability-sample inclusion and $\pi_i$ is its known
inclusion probability. The enriched sample may improve $m_i$; it does not
enter the default residual correction as though it represented the population.

Uncertainty must incorporate the exact human-sampling design, whole-issue
dependence in the substantive response design, and prediction-fitting or fold-
partition uncertainty when it is not explicitly conditioned away. Tables keep
five quantities distinct: original non-engagement, Sol machine-reference
sensitivity, direct probability-weighted human estimate, prediction-only
estimate and human-referenced corrected estimate. Frozen Sol probabilities may
serve as a provisional efficiency model for the current response population;
passing the cheaper-instrument gate is important for expansion but is not a
prerequisite for beginning this current-population correction.

The current probability sample contains no contributing human refusal event
for the China, EU or India standardized-home cells, one for MENA and two for
the US. Interval width alone is therefore not a sufficient readiness test: a
smooth prediction can appear precise despite no observed event in an arm. If
home remains a headline result, its targeted human design must be recalculated
after the final paper roster is fixed.

Adding subject models creates responses that had zero chance to enter the
current human probability sample. Old-roster results remain valid for that
roster, but expanded pooled or equal-model claims require a new probability
sample from the added rows, an external calibration audit and combination
under the actual multi-phase inclusion probabilities. The existing 700 labels
remain useful as a development corpus.

The defensible current conclusions are that the old measure mixes refusal and
capability failure, that the repaired target materially changes the internal
student assessment, that genuine-refusal prevalence now has a provisional
direct human-weighted estimate, that language remains imprecise, that no
low-cost population annotation instrument is externally certified, and that
home requires additional human event support. Enrichment percentages
are not prevalence; machine reference is not human truth; and a threshold
tuned and evaluated on the same fold cannot promote a candidate.
